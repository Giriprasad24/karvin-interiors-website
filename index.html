About Us

The insightful vision of our leader, Mr. K. Prasanna, together with the dedication of our professional management, architects, associates, engineers, managers and skilled workforce has played an important role in our growth since 1985.

We have executed interior contracts ranging from 2,000 to 1.60 lakh sq ft, adhering to high quality standards and time schedules.

Over the years, our experience has extended across luxury hotels, IT parks, corporate offices, banks, hospitals, retail spaces and residences.
