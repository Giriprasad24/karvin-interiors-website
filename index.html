About Karvin Interiors

The insightful vision of our leader, Mr. K. Prasanna, the dedication of our highly professional top management, our team of talented in-house architects/associates, engineers, managers and most importantly the skills of our motivated workforce, have played important roles in driving our growth since 1985.

We have executed interior contracts of sizeable magnitude varying from 2000 to 1.60 lakh sq. ft, adhering to high quality standards & time schedules.

Over the years, we have gained rich experience by executing contracts in various fields – luxury hotels, IT parks, corporate offices, banks, hospitals, retail, residences.
